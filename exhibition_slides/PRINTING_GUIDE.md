## 2. Browser Printing Instructions (1-Click 16:9 PDF Export)

To print the 10 slides for pinning onto your physical exhibition hardboard:

1. Open [`index.html`](file:///mnt/windows/AntiGravity/SCHOLARX/exhibition_slides/index.html) in **Google Chrome**, **Brave**, or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Press **<kbd>Ctrl + P</kbd>** (or click the green **Print Slides (PDF)** button in the top toolbar).
3. In the Print Dialog:
   - **Destination**: *Save as PDF* (or select your high-res color printer).
   - **Layout**: **Landscape**.
   - **Paper Size**: **A4** or **Letter** (or *Tabloid / A3* for large board displays).
   - **Margins**: **None** (*Critical:* setting margins to none ensures edge-to-edge alignment with zero clipping).
   - **Options**:
     - ✅ **Background graphics** (MUST BE CHECKED so the pure pitch-black canvas and crisp vector diagrams print).
     - ❌ **Headers and footers** (UNCHECK to remove page URLs and timestamps).
4. Click **Save** or **Print**. You will get 10 high-resolution pages, each formatted in standard 16:9 widescreen ratio with built-in push-pin alignment crosshairs (`+`) in all 4 corners.

## 3. Physical Paper & Hardboard Pin-Up Specifications

### Recommended Print Material:
- **Cardstock Weight**: **250 to 300 GSM** (Heavy matte or semi-gloss photo paper).
  * *Why:* Standard 80 GSM copy paper curls and sags when pinned on hardboard. 300 GSM cardstock stays flat, rigid, and executive-grade.
- **Finish**: **Satin / Semi-Matte** (Reduces glare from ceiling spotlights while keeping the pitch-black background deep and rich).
- **Push Pins**: Transparent or metallic thumb tacks inserted directly into the corner `+` crosshairs.
